Tomb of Knowledge

Tomb of Knowledge is an interactive digital library powered by the Open Library Book Search API. Visitors can search for books and explore details such as titles, authors, publication years and book covers, presented through interactive book cards. Unlike a basic search-results page, Tomb of Knowledge aims to recreate the experience of exploring a digital archive, making book discovery visually engaging and enjoyable.
